Forecasts on the record.

SekatApi joins NASA FIRMS hotspots, Open-Meteo weather, AWS terrain and OpenStreetMap into one peat-fire forecast, then hashes, signs and timestamps it on Bitcoin before the satellites see the outcome. Anyone can re-run the sealed inputs and get the same result. Every forecast stays on the board, good or bad.

Pre-registration

Sealing is three things.

Scroll to seal one forecast: a SHA-256 hash over every input and every simulated cell, an Ed25519 signature over that hash, and an OpenTimestamps anchor committed to Bitcoin. It happens before the outcome exists.

The record

Good or bad, it stays on the board.

Six forecasts were sealed on 21 Oct 2026 — three in Kalimantan, three in Sumatra — before their outcome existed. They are scored against NASA FIRMS after 24 h. None of them has been scored yet, and the board says so.

What sealing buys

A hindcast can be tuned until the past looks good. A pre-registered forecast cannot be: the inputs, the hash and the signature are fixed before the fire spreads.

Honest limits

What this is, and what it is not.

Is

A research prototype. A cellular-automaton fire simulation on a 6.4 × 6.4 km grid at 100 m cells, 40 runs per forecast, 24–48 h horizon. 47 automated tests pass. Re-running all 12 hindcasts a day later gives identical counts.

Is not

Not an operational fire-behaviour system and not firefighting guidance. The peat map outside OpenStreetMap features is still synthetic, so the emissions numbers are illustrative. The model's edge over a distance-only forecast is small — which is exactly why every forecast goes on the record.

Who it is for

Disaster managers and Manggala Agni commanders deciding where and when to act, provincial governments requesting aircraft, researchers comparing fire models, and auditors checking what was known and when. It runs in a browser on free open data; adding a region is a bounding box.
